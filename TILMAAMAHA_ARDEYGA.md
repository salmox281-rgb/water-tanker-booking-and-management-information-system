# Tilmaamaha Ardayga: Nidaamka Booyadaha Biyaha (Water Tanker MIS)
### Hagaha Tallaabo-Tallaabo ee Soo Bandhigidda Mashruuca Jaamacadda

Hambalyo! Mashruucaagii jaamacadeed ee **Water Tanker Booking and Management Information System** wuu diyaarsan yahay oo si buuxda ayuu u shaqeynayaa. Uma baahnid inaad hal xaraf oo code ah gacantaada ku qorto.

Halkan waxaan kuugu diyaariyey hagaha saxda ah ee aad raacayso:

---

## 💻 Tallaabada 1-aad: Sida aad Kombuyuutarkaaga ugu Furayso Hadda

1. Tag shaashadda kombuyuutarkaaga (Desktop-ka).
2. Furo folder-ka la yiraahdo **salmo x**.
3. Waxaad dhexdiisa ku arki doontaa fayl la yiraahdo **`index.html`**.
4. Labo jeer ku dhufo (Double-click) faylka **`index.html`**.
5. Wuxuu si toos ah kuugu furmayaa Browser-kaaga (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge ama Firefox).
6. Website-ku wuu shaqaynayaa xitaa haddii aadan internet haysan!

---

## 🎯 Tallaabada 2-aad: Meelaha aad Riixayso Marka aad Macallinka Tusinayso (Presentation Flow)

Marka aad hortagto macallinkaaga ama guddiga jaamacadda, sidan u tus tallaabo-tallaabo:

### 1. Bogga Hore (Home Page):
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** Shaashadda sare ee quruxda badan oo muujinaysa magaca nidaamka, noocyada biyaha, iyo cabbirrada booyadaha ($25 ilaa $120).
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix foomka degdegga ah ee dhanka midig ku yaal: gali magac (tusaale: *Cali Maxamed*) iyo taleefan, kadibna riix badhanka **"Xaqiiji & Dalbo Hadda"**.
- **Waxa dhacaya:** Wuxuu toos kuu geynayaa rasiid rasmi ah oo dalabkaas loo jaray!

### 2. Dashboard-ka Maamulka (Dashboard):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix liiska sare meesha ay ku qoran tahay **"Dashboard"**.
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** 
  - 6-da Kaar ee tirokoobka: Tirada Macaamiisha, Dalabaadka Guud, Kuwa La Gaarsiiyey, Kuwa Sugaya, Dakhliga Guud ($ USD), iyo Wadarta Litirada Biyaha.
  - Labada jaantus ee tooska ah: Jaantuska dakhliga (Revenue Line Chart) iyo kan xaaladaha (Status Doughnut Chart).
  - Shaxda 5-tii dalab ee ugu dambeeyey.

### 3. Maamulka Macaamiisha (Macaamiisha):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix menu-ga **"Macaamiisha"**.
- **Waxa aad sameynayso:**
  - Gali sanduuqa raadinta magac sida *"Axmed"* ama *"Kaxda"* si macallinku u arko raadinta degdegga ah (Instant Filter).
  - Riix badhanka buluugga ah ee **"Ku Dar Macmiil Cusub"** -> Geli magac iyo taleefan -> Riix "Kaydi Macmiilka".
  - Isla markiiba qofkaas wuxuu ku soo baxayaa shaxda!

### 4. Dalbashada Booyadda (Dalbo Booyad):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix menu-ga **"Dalbo Booyad"**.
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** 
  - Riix xulashada booyadaha (5,000L, 10,000L, 15,000L, 20,000L, 30,000L) -> Tus macallinka sida qiimaha kaarka midig ku yaal uu toos isu beddelayo ($25 -> $45 -> $85)!
  - Xulo macmiil hore, dooro degmada (Hodan, Wadajir, Kaxda, iwm), kadibna riix **"Diiwaangeli Dalabka Booyadda"**.

### 5. Maamulka Dalabaadka & Rasiidka (Dalabaadka):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix menu-ga **"Dalabaadka"**.
- **Waxa aad sameynayso:**
  - Riix badhanka **"Xaalad"** ee dalab kasta agtiisa yaal -> Ka dhig *Pending* una beddel *In Progress* ama *Delivered*.
  - Riix badhanka **"Rasiid"** -> Waxaa soo baxaya rasiid aad u qurux badan oo leh shabakadda biyaha, barcode, iyo shaambadda *PAID*.
  - Riix **"Daabac Rasiidka"** -> Wuxuu toos u furayaa daabacaha kombuyuutarka (Printer / Save as PDF).

### 6. Gaarsiinta Booyadaha (Gaarsiinta):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix **"Gaarsiinta"**.
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** Booyadaha waddada ku jira, darawallada iyo badhannada xaaladda booyadda looga dhigayo: *Diyaar*, *Waddada*, ama *Dayactir*.

### 7. Maamulka Lacagaha (Lacagaha):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix **"Lacagaha"**.
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** Dakhliga soo xarooday, hababka lacagta (EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal), iyo badhanka "Bixi Hadda" ee lacagaha harsan lagu bixinayo.

### 8. Warbixinnada (Warbixinnada):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix **"Warbixinnada"**.
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** Jaantusyada muujinaya noocyada booyadaha loogu jecel yahay iyo degmooyinka ugu dalabka badan, waxaadna riixi kartaa **"Soo deji Xogta (JSON)"** si kayd looga qaato.

### 9. Gelitaanka Maamulka (Login):
- **Waxa aad riixayso:** Riix **"Gelitaanka"** ama astaanta qufulka.
- **Waxaad tuseysaa:** Badhannada 1-Click demo ah ee **"Gali sida Maamule (Admin)"** iyo **"Gali sida Shaqaale (Staff)"**.

## 🌐 Tallaabada 3-aad: Sida aad Link Toos ah ugu Helayso (Internet-ka Bilaash Loogu Daabaco)

Si aad u hesho link aad macallinka ugu diri karto WhatsApp ama Email, waxaad isticmaalaysaa **Netlify Drop** (Waa adeeg bilaash ah oo aan lacag ama kaar bangi u baahnayn, qaadanayana 30 ilbiriqsi oo keliya):

1. Fur internet-ka oo gal cinwaankan: **[https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)**
2. Waxaad shaashadda ku arki doontaa sanduuq ay ku qoran tahay *"Drag and drop your site output folder here"*.
3. Soo qabo folder-kaaga **`salmo x`** ee Desktop-ka ku yaal, adigoo jiidaya (Drag) ku dhex rid sanduuqaas Netlify.
4. Sug 10 ilbiriqsi inta uu soo gelinayo.
5. Isla goobta Netlify wuxuu ku siinayaa Link toos ah (tusaale: `https://water-tanker-somalia-xxx.netlify.app`).
6. Link-gaas ayaad u diri kartaa macallinkaaga, asxaabtaada, waxaadna ka furi kartaa telefoonkaaga gacanta!

## 🎓 Tallaabada 4-aad: Su'aalaha Macallinku ku Weydiin Karo & Jawaabahooda

**1. Su'aasha: Xogtu xaggee ku kaydsan tahay?**
- **Jawaabtaada:** *"Mudane macallin, nidaamkan wajigiisan koowaad waxa uu xogta ku kaydiyaa kaydka browser-ka ee loo yaqaan `localStorage`. Tani waxay suurtogelinaysaa in nidaamku si buuxda u shaqeeyo isagoo offline ah oo bilaa server lacag ah ah, xogtuna ma lumayso xitaa haddii bogga la cusboonaysiiyo. Wajiga labaadna waxa loo diyaariyey in toos loogu xiro database SQL ah sida MySQL ama PostgreSQL."*

**2. Su'aasha: Waa maxay tiknoolajiyada aad u isticmaashay frontend-ka?**
- **Jawaabtaada:** *"Waxaan isticmaalay HTML5 semantik ah, Tailwind CSS iyo CSS3 casri ah oo loogu talagalay responsiveness-ka iyo naqshadda, JavaScript ES6 oo loo adeegsaday shaqada nidaamka (CRUD logic), iyo Chart.js oo loo adeegsaday soo saarista jaantusyada tirokoobka."*

**3. Su'aasha: Nidaamku telefoonka ma ka shaqaynayaa?**
- **Jawaabtaada:** *"Haa, nidaamku waa 100% Mobile Responsive. Waxa uu leeyahay Mobile Drawer Navigation iyo shaxado si habsami leh ugu habboon shaashadaha yaryar iyo kuwa waaweynba."*
